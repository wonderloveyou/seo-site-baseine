# On-page, content, trust, and conversion

## Page worksheet

For each sampled page record evidence ID, URL, template, audience, dominant intent, title, description, H1, opening answer or offer, decision-relevant sections, factual support, trust signals, internal paths, CTA, and observation limitations.

Check whether the page:

- makes the audience, value, and next useful action explicit;
- uses accurate, distinctive metadata and headings aligned with the visible page;
- answers the dominant intent early and covers necessary decisions, objections, constraints, and alternatives;
- supports consequential claims with traceable facts, dates, methods, examples, and accountable authorship;
- uses headings, tables, lists, links, and media to improve comprehension and accessibility;
- offers a proportionate CTA and explains the next step without fabricated urgency or proof.

Prefer first-party facts, primary documentation, regulators or standards, peer-reviewed work, and transparent datasets. Record dates and methods for numeric claims. Preserve unresolved source conflicts.

Evaluate completeness and usefulness rather than universal word counts, keyword density, LSI quotas, fixed metadata lengths, image-per-word ratios, or uniqueness scores. Headings describe useful subtopics rather than keyword slots.

Treat E-E-A-T as a lens for truthful accountability, not a numeric checklist. Review authorship, organization and contact details, editorial policy, sources, real cases, and update dates. Use FAQ only for genuine questions; its presence or markup does not prove enhanced search or AI visibility.

## Roll-up rule

Tag every observation `url`, `template`, or `sitewide`. Consolidate only after repeated evidence or inspection of the shared implementation. Keep isolated page defects separate from shared-template fixes.

## Module output

Return the sampled-page worksheets or their artifact locator, evidence IDs, per-page observations, roll-up breadth, repeated patterns, exceptions, and the consolidated candidates that enter the findings table.
